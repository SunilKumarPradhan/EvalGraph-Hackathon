# Brewing

Espresso uses 18 grams of coffee and brews for 25 to 30 seconds at 9 bar. A shorter time means the grind is too coarse.

The lungo button runs 110 ml through the same dose. It tastes more bitter because of the longer extraction.

Milk is frothed by the steam wand at 140 degrees Celsius. Purge the wand for two seconds before and after frothing.

The cup warmer on top reaches 50 degrees after ten minutes and switches off with the machine.
