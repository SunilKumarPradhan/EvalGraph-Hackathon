# Setup

Unpack the Orbit and remove the orange transport clip from under the drip tray before switching it on.

Fill the water tank with fresh, cold water up to the MAX line. Filtered water reduces scale build-up.

Hold the power button for three seconds. The ring light pulses white while the boiler heats, which takes about 45 seconds.

On first use, run two empty cycles with the cup sensor covered to flush the internal lines.
