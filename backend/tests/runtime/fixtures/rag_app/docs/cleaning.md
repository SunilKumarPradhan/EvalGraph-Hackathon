# Cleaning

Empty the drip tray and the grounds container daily. Both are dishwasher safe on the top rack.

Descale every 200 cycles or when the ring light turns orange. Use only citric-acid descaler; vinegar damages the seals.

Wipe the steam wand with a damp cloth right after every use so milk does not dry inside the tip.

Replace the water filter cartridge every two months.
